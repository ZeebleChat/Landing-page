# PRODUCT.md — Zeeble

## Product Purpose
Zeeble is a modern chat platform that combines real-time messaging, crystal-clear voice, and a universal "Beam Identity" that works across both self-hosted and cloud servers. It's built as a privacy-respecting, user-controlled alternative to Discord with a focus on identity portability and community-driven features like server boosts and public emojis.

## Target Users
- **Communities**: Gaming groups, open-source projects, friend circles, professional teams
- **Tech-savvy users**: Those who value self-hosting, data ownership, and platform independence
- **Content creators**: Streamers, YouTubers, developers who want a customizable community space
- **Premium enthusiasts**: Users willing to pay $5/month for enhanced features and to support the ecosystem

## Brand Personality
- **Bold but not brash**: Confident, forward-thinking, professional yet approachable
- **Transparent and honest**: "No hidden costs, no data mining, no compromise"
- **Community-first**: Features like public emojis and boost tiers emphasize collective ownership
- **Technical but accessible**: Powers users with control without requiring expertise

## Unique Value Propositions
1. **Beam Identity**: One identity that follows you across every Zeeble server—self-hosted or cloud
2. **True self-hosting**: PhaseLink gives admins full control, unlimited users, zero telemetry
3. **10 free cloud servers**: No credit card, no trial—just create and go
4. **Message search for everyone**: Not locked behind paywall
5. **Server boosts unlock public emojis**: Community-driven feature unlocks

## Anti-References (What We're NOT)
- ❌ NOT Discord clone—we're a respectful alternative with our own philosophy
- ❌ NOT corporate SaaS with dark patterns—transparent, honest pricing
- ❌ NOT "AI-washed"—real features, no buzzword bingo
- ❌ NOT skeuomorphic or playful like early messenger apps—modern, confident, digital-native
- ❌ NOT minimal-to-the-point-of-cold—warmth through purple/zurple accents, not generic blue

## Strategic Principles
- **Freedom first**: Users control their data, identity, and community
- **Sustainable economics**: Premium at $5/mo is fair; boosts align incentives
- **Open infrastructure**: GitHub repos for client & server; community contributions welcome
- **Respectful growth**: No engagement hacks, no dark patterns, no telemetry
